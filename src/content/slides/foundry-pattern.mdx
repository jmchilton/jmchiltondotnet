---

{/* SLIDE 16 — KB */}

<section>

<div class="text-sm opacity-50" style="letter-spacing: 0.18em;">01 · SOURCE OF TRUTH</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 1.45em;">Knowledge Base</h2>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1em;">
  Plain files, authored to be <strong>read and learned by a person</strong>.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.66em; margin-top: 1.1em; opacity: 0.85;">
  Typed frontmatter · controlled tags · wiki-linked references
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.4em;">
  "Executable" is a claim the build enforces: every typed reference must resolve, every tag must exist.<br />
  A Mold whose source fails these checks cannot be cast.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 17 — Mold */}

<section>

<div class="text-sm opacity-50" style="letter-spacing: 0.18em;">02 · ONE ACTION</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 1.45em;">Mold</h2>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1em;">
  A typed reference manifest plus a procedural skeleton.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.62em; margin-top: 1.3em; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  Every declared reference carries three decisions:
  <ul style="margin-top: 0.5em;">
    <li><strong>kind</strong> — which resolver and casting rules apply</li>
    <li><strong>load policy</strong> — upfront, or on-demand with a trigger</li>
    <li><strong>placement</strong> — verbatim, or sidecar</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.1em;">
  One coherent unit of work. Not an entire journey, not a dust of fragments.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 18 — Cast */}

<section>

<div class="text-sm opacity-50" style="letter-spacing: 0.18em;">03 · COMPILATION</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 1.45em;">Cast</h2>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1em;">
  The same source, Mold, and target produce <strong>the same bytes</strong>.
</div>

<div style="display: flex; justify-content: center; gap: 1.6em; margin-top: 1.5em; font-size: 0.66em;">
  <div><strong style="color: #2563eb;">scoped</strong><br /><span class="text-sm opacity-70">only what this action declares</span></div>
  <div><strong style="color: #2563eb;">isolated</strong><br /><span class="text-sm opacity-70">links resolved away, no runtime tie</span></div>
  <div><strong style="color: #2563eb;">frozen</strong><br /><span class="text-sm opacity-70">one revision, not future edits</span></div>
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.5em;">
  The skill body is never hand-maintained. Under-instructed? Fix the Mold and cast again.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 19 — Provenance */}

<section>

<div class="text-sm opacity-50" style="letter-spacing: 0.18em;">04 · LINEAGE RECORD</div>

<h2 style="font-size: 1.45em;">Provenance</h2>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1em;">
  Which Mold revision. Which target. Which references resolved. Which checks ran.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.8em; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.6em; border-left: 7px solid #dc2626; padding-left: 0.65em; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  Not "the model probably knew this" —<br />
  <em>this claim is real, and here is where it came from.</em>
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 20 — the analogy */}

<section>

<h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.8em;">The picture to hold</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.78em; line-height: 1.9; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  the skill is the <strong style="color: #2563eb;">binary</strong><br />
  the KB is the <strong style="color: #2563eb;">code</strong><br />
  casting is the <strong style="color: #2563eb;">compiler</strong><br />
  provenance is the <strong style="color: #2563eb;">build log</strong>
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.68em; margin-top: 1.6em;">
  No serious engineering culture treats the compiled binary<br />as the place you make changes.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 21 — targets / portability */}

<section>

<h3>Targets change the package, not the source</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.7em; margin-top: 1.3em;">
  One Mold → Claude skill, generic agent, baked-in prompt.
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.5em;">
  Agent platforms will change faster than the domain knowledge they consume.<br />
  Binding source knowledge to today's packaging format makes it inherit the platform's lifetime.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 23 — the experiment */}

<section>

<h3>Blind regeneration</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.68em; margin-top: 1.1em; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  Delete a science skill. Hand a frontier model only <strong>the topic and the format</strong>.<br />
  How much of the original does it write back from memory?
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.6em; margin-top: 1.4em; opacity: 0.85; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  A separate blind-author agent — never shown the original.<br />
  Hard prohibition: no reading the corpus, no web search.<br />
  Two skills at different altitudes: one light and procedural, one decision-grade.
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.3em;">
  Designed so it could fail. High overlap ⇒ the restatement is redundant.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 24 — results */}

<section>

<h3>It wrote the skill back</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.6em; margin-top: 1.1em; text-align: left; display: inline-block; line-height: 1.7;">
  <strong>Canonical spine + default parameters</strong> — reproduced verbatim<br />
  <span class="opacity-70">PCA n_comps=50 · n_neighbors=15 · resolution 0.2–2.0 · Leiden over Louvain</span><br /><br />
  <strong>The decision-grade page</strong> — 85–90% recovered, roughly a wash<br /><br />
  <strong>The light page</strong> — <span style="color: #dc2626; font-weight: 700;">the blind version was better</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.66em; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.4em; border-left: 7px solid #dc2626; padding-left: 0.65em; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  The redundancy effect is strongest exactly where corpora are thickest.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 25 — the citation finding */}

<section>

<h3>It reproduced the citations too</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1.2em;">
  From the weights. Including the fuzzy ones.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.8em; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5em; border-left: 7px solid #dc2626; padding-left: 0.65em; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  You cannot tell, from the output,<br />which of those are correct.
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.4em;">
  A real citation and a confabulated one look identical on the page.<br />
  A smarter model produces <em>more convincing</em> unverifiable citations, not more verifiable ones.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 26 — the finding that reframes everything */}

<section>

<h3>And it added <em>more</em> validity caveats than the original</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.7em; margin-top: 1.3em;">
  We expected it to be thinner on rigor. It volunteered cautions the original never mentioned.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1.5em;">
  So validity <strong>knowledge</strong> is already in the weights.
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.3em;">
  Which forces the obvious objection: then what does a Foundry add that the weights do not?
</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 28 — the two assets */}

<section>

<h3>The two things it could not supply</h3>

<div style="display: grid; grid-template-columns: repeat(2, minmax(0, 1fr)); gap: 1.2em; margin-top: 1.3em; font-size: 0.62em; text-align: left;">
  <div style="border-left: 5px solid #2563eb; padding-left: 0.8em;">
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 1.25em;">Provenance</div>
    <div style="opacity: 0.75; margin-top: 0.2em;">the universal asset</div>
    <div style="margin-top: 0.7em;">Which claim is real, and where it came from. Rides with every cast, in every domain.</div>
  </div>
  <div style="border-left: 5px solid #dc2626; padding-left: 0.8em;">
    <div style="font-weight: 700; font-size: 1.25em;">An enforced check</div>
    <div style="opacity: 0.75; margin-top: 0.2em;">realized per domain</div>
    <div style="margin-top: 0.7em;">An external verdict between authored knowledge and a trusted result — not an optional caveat.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.66em; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.4em;">
  Restating canonical knowledge is a commodity.<br />
  Traceable, check-backed knowledge is not.
</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 30 — the three instances, and what varies */}

<section>

<h3>Three domains, one substrate</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.58em; margin-top: 1.2em; text-align: left; display: inline-block; line-height: 1.8;">
  <strong>Galaxy Workflow Foundry</strong> — papers, Nextflow, CWL → validated Galaxy workflows<br />
  <span class="opacity-70">check: a deterministic CLI, gxwf — "the rails"</span><br /><br />
  <strong>TDA Bioinformatics Foundry</strong> — topological methods → reproducible tools<br />
  <span class="opacity-70">check: evolving — reproducible environments, evidence-bearing replication</span><br /><br />
  <strong>Statistical Genomics Foundry</strong> — keeping an agent statistically honest<br />
  <span class="opacity-70">check: an empirical referee — analyze → referee → revise</span>
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.3em;">
  Provenance is universal. <strong>The check belongs to the domain.</strong>
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 31 — why the check has to be external */}

<section>

<h3>Deterministic tools do deterministic work</h3>

<div style="font-size: 0.72em; margin-top: 1.2em;">
  A prose caveat is advisory. A model may repeat one and still violate it.
</div>

<div style="font-size: 0.82em; font-weight: 700; margin-top: 1.5em; border-left: 7px solid #dc2626; padding-left: 0.65em; text-align: left; display: inline-block;">
  Most importantly:<br />doing does not get to certify itself.
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.4em;">
  A parser where correctness is mechanical.<br />
  An empirical referee — simulation, calibration, negative controls — where it isn't.
</div>

</section>

---

{/* SLIDE 32 — close */}

<section>

<h2 style="font-size: 1.4em; line-height: 1.3;">
  Ship skills.<br />
  <span style="color: #2563eb;">Author knowledge.</span>
</h2>

<div style="font-size: 0.68em; margin-top: 1.3em;">
  Cast them into every runtime you like. But author the knowledge somewhere<br />
  a human can read it and an agent can trace it, back it with a check that actually fires,<br />
  and keep the receipt.
</div>

<div class="text-sm opacity-70" style="margin-top: 1.6em;">
  The artifact layer is a commodity headed toward zero margin.<br />
  The source layer and the enforced check above it are where the durable value lives.
</div>
