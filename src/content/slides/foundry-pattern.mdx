{/* SLIDE 7 — KB */}

<section class="vstack">

<div class="eyebrow">01 · Source of truth</div>

<h2>Knowledge Base</h2>

<div class="lede">Plain files, authored to be <strong>read and learned by a person</strong>.</div>

<div class="chain">
  <span>typed frontmatter</span>
  <span class="chain-sep">·</span>
  <span>controlled tags</span>
  <span class="chain-sep">·</span>
  <span>wiki-linked references</span>
</div>

<a class="ref-link" href="https://gist.github.com/karpathy/442a6bf555914893e9891c11519de94f">
  Karpathy — llm-wiki.md ↗
</a>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 8 — Mold */}

<section class="vstack">

<div class="eyebrow">02 · One action</div>

<h2>Mold</h2>

<div class="lede">A typed reference manifest plus a procedural skeleton.</div>

<div class="body">Every declared reference carries three decisions:</div>

<div class="cards cards-3 measure-lg">
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-title">kind</div>
    <div class="card-body">which resolver and casting rules apply</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-gold">
    <div class="card-title">load policy</div>
    <div class="card-body">upfront, or on-demand with a trigger</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-grey">
    <div class="card-title">placement</div>
    <div class="card-body">verbatim, or sidecar</div>
  </div>
</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 9 — Cast */}

<section class="vstack">

<div class="eyebrow">03 · Compilation</div>

<h2>Cast</h2>

<div class="lede">Compile the references and procedure described by the Mold into a skill.</div>

<div class="cards cards-3 measure-lg">
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-title">scoped</div>
    <div class="card-body">only what this action declares</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-gold">
    <div class="card-title">isolated</div>
    <div class="card-body">links resolved away, no runtime tie</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-grey">
    <div class="card-title">frozen</div>
    <div class="card-body">one revision, not future edits</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="note">
  The skill is never hand-maintained. Under-instructed? Fix the Mold and/or its references and
  cast again. Prefer thin Mold procedures and rich references to maximize reuse.
</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 10 — Provenance */}

<section class="vstack">

<div class="eyebrow">04 · Lineage record</div>

<h2>Provenance</h2>

<div class="lede">Which Mold revision. Which target. Which references resolved. Which checks ran.</div>

<div class="callout">
  Not "the model probably knew this" —<br />
  <em>this claim is real, and here is where it came from.</em>
</div>

</section>

# An Appeal with Evidence

<div class="lede">We didn't assert it. We tried to falsify it.</div>

{/* SLIDE 28 — the experiment */}

<section class="vstack">

<h3>Blind regeneration</h3>

<div class="lede">
  Delete a science skill. Hand a frontier model only <strong>the topic and the format</strong>.
  How much of the original does it write back from memory?
</div>

<div class="cards cards-3 measure-lg">
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-body">A separate blind-author agent — never shown the original.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-gold">
    <div class="card-body">Hard prohibition: no reading the corpus, no web search.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-grey">
    <div class="card-body">Two skills at different altitudes: one light and procedural, one decision-grade.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="note">Designed so it could fail. High overlap ⇒ the restatement is redundant.</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 29 — results */}

<section class="vstack">

<h3>It wrote the skill back</h3>

<div class="cards cards-3 measure-lg">
  <div class="card">
    <div class="card-title">Canonical spine + default parameters</div>
    <div class="card-body">reproduced verbatim</div>
    <div class="card-note" style="margin-top: 8px;">PCA n_comps=50 · n_neighbors=15 · resolution 0.2–2.0 · Leiden over Louvain</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-gold">
    <div class="card-title">The decision-grade page</div>
    <div class="card-body">85–90% recovered, roughly a wash</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-warn">
    <div class="card-title">The light page</div>
    <div class="card-body"><strong class="hi-warn">the blind version was better</strong></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="callout">The redundancy effect is strongest exactly where corpora are thickest.</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 30 — the citation finding */}

<section class="vstack">

<h3>It reproduced the citations too</h3>

<div class="lede">From the weights. Including the fuzzy ones.</div>

<div class="callout callout-warn">
  You cannot tell, from the output,<br />which of those are correct.
</div>

<div class="note">
  A real citation and a confabulated one look identical on the page.<br />
  A smarter model produces <em>more convincing</em> unverifiable citations, not more verifiable ones.
</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 31 — the finding that reframes everything */}

<section class="vstack">

<h3 class="plain-heading measure">And it added <em>more</em> validity caveats than the original</h3>

<div class="lede">We expected it to be thinner on rigor. It volunteered cautions the original never mentioned.</div>

<div class="statement statement-sm">So validity <strong>knowledge</strong> is already in the weights.</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 32 — the two assets */}

<section class="vstack">

<h3>The two things it could not supply</h3>

<div class="cards cards-2 measure">
  <div class="card card-side">
    <div class="card-title card-title-lg">Provenance</div>
    <div class="card-eyebrow" style="margin-top: 4px;">the universal asset</div>
    <div class="card-body">Which claim is real, and where it came from. Rides with every cast, in every domain.</div>
  </div>
  <div class="card card-side card-gold">
    <div class="card-title card-title-lg">An enforced check</div>
    <div class="card-eyebrow" style="margin-top: 4px;">realized per domain</div>
    <div class="card-body">An external verdict between authored knowledge and a trusted result — not an optional caveat.</div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="statement statement-sm">
  Restating canonical knowledge is a commodity.<br />
  Traceable, check-backed knowledge is not.
</div>

</section>

<div class="eyebrow">Part 2.4</div>

# The Case for Resisting Rot

</section>

{/* SLIDE 34 — newer models obsolete generated skills */}

<section class="vstack">

<h2 class="plain-heading" style="font-size: 1.38em; line-height: 1.18;">
  The generated skill is<br /><span class="hi">the perishable layer.</span>
</h2>

<div class="body">The better blind version is the clue: generated skill quality rises with model capability.</div>

<div class="statement statement-sm">Who cares what Opus 4.6 wrote after Opus 5 ships?</div>

<div class="note">
  A skill frozen in place inherits the limits of the model that authored it.<br />
  Keep the source, and recast.
</div>

</section>

{/* SLIDE 35 — targets / portability */}

<section class="vstack">

<h3>Targets change the package, not the source</h3>

<div class="lede">One Mold → Claude skill, generic agent, baked-in prompt.</div>

<div class="note">
  Agent platforms will change faster than the domain knowledge they consume.<br />
  Binding source knowledge to today's packaging format makes it inherit the platform's lifetime.
</div>

</section>
