{/* SLIDE 2 — what the pattern adds to a knowledge base */}

<section class="vstack">

<h2>Making the knowledge base actionable</h2>

<div class="body measure">
  Karpathy-style knowledge bases are great at <strong>storing and organizing</strong> what a project knows.
</div>

<div class="callout">
  The Foundry Pattern structures <strong>deterministic slices</strong> of that base into the context needed
  to <strong>perform an action</strong>.
</div>
